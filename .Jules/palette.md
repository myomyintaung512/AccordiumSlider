## 2026-03-24 - Decoupling Element Reset from Row State in Custom jQuery Galleries
**Learning:** In highly interactive custom JavaScript/jQuery accordion galleries, applying styles (like borders or custom margins) on focus/hover must be managed per-element rather than tied to the row's general state. If the reset logic of individual sibling items is nested inside a container-wide check (such as whether any sibling remains active), moving focus directly between adjacent sibling items will fail to trigger the reset for the previously active item, leading to stuck visual states (like double borders).
**Action:** Always decouple individual element visual resets from global container or row-wide state checks. Ensure each interactive item triggers its own style cleanups immediately on blur or mouseleave, independent of whether other items in the container are still active.
