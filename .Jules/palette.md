## 2026-07-24 - Synchronized Hover & Focus in Shared-Preview Galleries
**Learning:** In shared-preview galleries where multiple items target a single expansion area, utilizing separate `.data('hovered')` and `.data('focused')` flags combined with a debounced (10ms) deactivation check is far more robust than relying on mouseenter/mouseleave or simple focusout/e.relatedTarget, as it cleanly coordinates sequential keyboard navigation, mouse hover transitions, and nested links.
**Action:** When designing accordion-like or shared expansion zones, manage state with discrete flags (e.g., `.data('hovered')`, `.data('focused')`) on each element, and debounce state changes to prevent premature closing animations when focus transitions between sibling items or internal interactive links.
