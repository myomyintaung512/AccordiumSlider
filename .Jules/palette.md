## 2026-08-01 - [Synchronized Keyboard and Mouse Hover Coordination]
**Learning:** In shared-preview jQuery galleries, coordination of mouse and keyboard focus interactions on child elements requires separate internal tracking flags (e.g., `hovered` and `focused`) to ensure fluid transitions. Simply relying on focus and hover toggles without a unified debouncing state check results in premature collapse or visual flickering, especially when sequential keyboard focus navigates through nested interactive children (like anchor tags) inside the parent container.
**Action:** Always maintain explicit data attributes or state flags (`hovered`, `focused`) on the interactive containers, and utilize a small timeout check before executing deactivation animations to ensure no sibling box or nested link remains active.
